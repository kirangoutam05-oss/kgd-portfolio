DROP FILES HERE
===============

One folder per packaging group. Put whatever you have into the matching
folder - artwork, dielines, mockups, shelf photos. Format does not
matter: AI, PSD, PDF, INDD exports, JPG, PNG, TIFF all work, and they
get rendered locally.

  coriander      Coriander 1Kg, Seeds 5Kg A1, 5Kg Mango, 10Kg A1, 10Kg Mango
  rice           9 Basmati grades, Jeera Samba 1Kg, Sonam 26Kg, Vada Kolam 26Kg
  jeera          Jeera Seeds 1Kg, 5Kg Premium, 5Kg Supreme
  mustard-oil    250ml, 500ml, 1L
  soya-chunks    220gm, Rs10, Mini Rs10
  dals           Channa Dal 26Kg (+ Toor Dal if it exists)
  atta-sooji     Atta 30Kg, Sooji 500gm
  crispy-corner  Corn Flakes Rs10, Garlic Mixture Rs10
  vedhaa         Ghee 100ml / 450ml / 900ml, plus the oil

Alongside the files, a NOTES.txt in each folder - two or three lines is
plenty:

  - what the brief was
  - what constrained it (print process, substrate, shelf, the
    regulatory panel, an existing range it had to sit beside)
  - what you decided, and what that cost

Images show what the work looks like. They do not show why it looks
that way, and the why is what makes a case study worth reading.

Nothing needs to arrive at once. A single finished folder is enough to
start on.
