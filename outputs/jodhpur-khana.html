Jodhpur
Khana

Jodhpur Khana is an initiative to attract tourists to new, authentic cuisine and local dishes of Jodhpur — designed to direct customers to a mobile application that helps them locate these specialities, while providing relevant functionality for local sellers.

UX ResearchUI Design 3D DesignDesign ResearchExperiential Design
PlaceJodhpur, Rajasthan
ForTourists and street vendors
OutputIdentity, app, pop-up stall
Overview

An app for finding the vendor, and a stall that points back to it.

Jodhpur Khana directs visitors to the city’s authentic local dishes — and to the informal street vendors who actually make them. The app handles discovery, listings, maps and payments; a modular pop-up stall gives the initiative a presence on the street that an app alone cannot have.

The app

Four screens, each doing one job.

The stall

A modular cart, and the standee that joins the two halves.

The standee carries the mark at street scale and a QR code that opens the app — so a tourist who walks past a stall ends up holding the directory.
