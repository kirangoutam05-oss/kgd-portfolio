01 / OVERVIEW

Jodhpur Khana is an initiative to attract tourists to authentic, local dishes across Jodhpur — while giving the informal street vendors who make that food a real channel to be discovered and paid fairly for it.

The project is designed to direct customers to a mobile application that helps tourists locate these specialties, while also providing relevant functionality for the local sellers behind them — the "vulnerable group" this project centres isn't the tourist, it's the small vendor who'd otherwise have no visibility beyond their own street corner.
