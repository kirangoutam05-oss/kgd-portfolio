& The Mixer

Building a brand identity, developing a brand strategy and growing a loyal customer base for Trippy Goat, in the heart of Bangalore, attracting customers drawn to fusion cuisine paired with wine blends from around the world — and the event brand, The Mixer, that grew out of it.

Overview

One cafe, one wine list, and an event brand that outgrew the room.

Trippy Goat is a cafe and wine bar on Hatworks Boulevard, off Cunningham Road in Bengaluru. The work ran from the mark itself through menus, packaging, photography and social, then into The Mixer — a networking event series the cafe hosted, which needed its own identity and campaign.

Around the Mediterranean

A chef's table, worked through in full.

One evening from the programme, shown end to end: Around the Mediterranean with Chef Natasha Celmi, 19 March 2021. The invitation leads with the food against a deep teal ground; the menu inverts it, near-white, with illustrated motifs stepped back so the six courses carry the page.

Barcelona Tapas Bar, Tropical Puglia, Moroccan Spice, Greek Island Hopping, An Italian in Athens, La Dolce Vita — each course named for where it lands rather than what it is. Produced with Tinn Ginni as communication partner, Fratelli as wine partner, and Hyper Grocer among the ingredient partners.
