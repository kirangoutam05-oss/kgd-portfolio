Logofolio

A series of logo designs for digital assets as well as product based assets — four food and lifestyle brands, each worked through from wordmark to application.

Digital DesignBrandingGraphic Design
BrandsMeraki, Swastha, Marin, Orinko
ScopeWordmarks and sub-brands
FormatNine-page collection
Overview

Four brands, four different problems a wordmark had to solve.

Each identity here answers a different brief. Meraki needed a brand system elastic enough to carry sub-brands; Swastha needed a mark that reads as calm rather than clinical; Marin needed heritage without pastiche; Orinko needed to look like fresh produce rather than packaged food.

  • MerakiAn FMCG brand in coffee and honey, repositioned for a younger audience — with meraki agro and Copper Bean as sub-brands.
  • SwasthaA softly drawn wordmark, set across three weights and a sage palette.
  • MarinA premium grape brandy, drawing on Indian maritime and ship illustration.
  • OrinkoAn organic produce brand, made approachable rather than worthy.
