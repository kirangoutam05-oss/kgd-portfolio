Forum
by ReGo

Forum by ReGo is a platform that brings multi-disciplinary creatives into a single space, allowing individuals to showcase their work and create an opportunity to interact with the community.

Marketing StrategyMotion Graphics BrandingSocial Media Strategy
ClientReGo
ScopeIdentity, events, go-to-market
Format18-page strategy document
Overview

Three things had to exist at once: creatives, a space, and a community.

Forum's proposition rests on three pillars the document defines up front — creatives, a place for any creative individual to showcase ongoing projects or school assignments; space, a place to platform in any community; and community, a strong and inclusive creative community across a spectrum of creatives, individuals, enthusiasts, experts and students.

The work runs from a rectified brand name and mark through to event guidelines, a marketing strategy and a roadmap — structured around a framework the document calls the Rising Formula.

  • Set UpBrand mission and vision, the rectified name, and the mark.
  • Ramp UpMarketing strategy, word of mouth, promotional campaign.
  • Show UpEvent guidelines, social engagement, the Wall of Networking.
  • Keep UpFeedback systems, CRM activity, post-event advertising.
